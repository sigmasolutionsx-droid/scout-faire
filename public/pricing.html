Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as an "analysis"?

Each time you submit a niche for analysis, that counts as one search. Elite gets unlimited (fair use 500/month), Oracle gets 2,000/month. You can re-analyze the same niche multiple times if needed.

What is "Fair Use" and why do you have it?

Fair Use limits prevent abuse while giving you generous access. Elite averages 16 analyses/day, Oracle averages 66/day. If you legitimately need more for agency use, contact us for enterprise pricing.

What's the difference between Elite and Oracle?

Elite shows you WHAT gaps exist in the market. Oracle shows you HOW TO MAKE MONEY from those gaps - with specific pricing models, revenue streams, and positioning strategies. Oracle is for people ready to execute, not just research.

What's included in competitor gaps analysis?

We identify 5-7 specific opportunities your competitors are missing. Elite tier shows what's missing, pain points, and opportunity sizing. Oracle tier adds detailed business models showing exactly how to monetize each gap.
