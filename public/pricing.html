Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "search" or "analysis"?

Each time you submit a niche for analysis, that counts as one search. You can analyze as many niches as your plan allows per month.

What is "Fair Use" and why do you have it?

Fair Use limits prevent abuse while giving you generous access. Pro gets 500 analyses/month (16/day average), Enterprise gets 2,000/month (66/day average). If you legitimately need more, contact us for a custom plan.

What's included in competitor gaps analysis?

We identify 5-7 specific opportunities your competitors are missing, including what's missing in the market, customer pain points, execution difficulty ratings, revenue opportunity sizing, and actionable recommendations.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes! Upgrade instantly to access more features. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with Scout-Faire within the first week, contact us for a full refund.
