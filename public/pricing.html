Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Fair Use: 500/mo" mean?

It means unlimited for normal business use - up to 500 comprehensive analyses per month. This generous limit protects our service from automated abuse while giving you more than enough capacity for serious market research. 99% of users never hit this limit.

What are "Competitor Gaps"?

Our AI identifies 5-7 specific unmet needs in your niche that existing competitors are missing. Each gap includes what's missing, customer pain points, revenue opportunity, and execution difficulty - giving you a clear roadmap to differentiate your business.
