Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get my website and email set up?
Free hosting, WordPress, and email are included with Founders Pack and Enterprise. Please allow up to 72 hours after signup for your website and email to be provisioned and ready. You'll receive an email with your login details and setup instructions once everything is live.
What is the Founders Pack 30-day Enterprise trial?
When you sign up for the Founders Pack, your first 30 days are automatically upgraded to full Enterprise — no extra charge. You get Scout AI full coaching, asset generation, business plan PDF, the end-of-month interview, everything. On day 31 you continue at $49.98/mo with Founders Pack features, locked forever.
Why is the Enterprise Annual founders price $797?
That's $66.42/mo — only $16.44 more than the Founders Pack paid annually. For that $16 you get the entire Freedom Uprise platform as it grows: Daedalus, Trail-Maker, Shintaku, Aetherium, Arcus, Maximus — every tool we build, included. In 3 years when this platform is worth $200/mo you'll still be paying $66.42.
Is the founders price really locked forever?
Yes. A deal is a deal. Founders who lock in today keep that rate for as long as they stay subscribed. As the platform grows and prices increase, your rate never changes. We honor every commitment we make.
What is Scout AI?
Scout is the AI coach built into Scout-Faire. It knows your niche report, your competitor gaps, your offer, and your 30-day plan. Founders Pack gets process-based guidance — Scout tells you which of the 5 processes you're in and what's left to complete it. Enterprise gets full daily coaching — Scout tells you exactly what to do today, writes assets on demand, and clusters tasks around your real-life situation.
What's coming to the platform?
Daedalus (AI product builder), Trail-Maker (funnel builder), Shintaku Oracle (AI email sequences), Aetherium (WhatsApp campaigns), Arcus CRM, and Maximus (social media growth) are all built and being deployed. Enterprise founders get every tool as it launches — no upgrade fees, no extra charges, ever.
Can I cancel?
Yes, cancel anytime. If you cancel your founders pricing is not held — if you resubscribe later you'll pay the current market rate. We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first payment.
